## Before

### How do you plan to begin this assignment?
Step 1: Write out the type rules on paper to ensure some consistency in my program's
design and implementation. I have attempted to go right to coding before and usually
have to go back and redesign my projects. With this being a language I've never done
anything substantial with, I think a solid design is necessary.

Following, I hope to work from the bottom up, meaning the simplest and most necessary
code will be written and tested first. From looking at the assignment, that includes
variable type lookup, checking for a name existing in the environment, new bindings,
and updates to existing bindings - in that order. These predicates make other rules
possible, so I'll put a lot of focus here in the beginning.

Next, I think type compatibility could be implemented. With foundational rules
written and functioning properly, these rules shouldn't be too difficult to write and
test. Following this, expressions, mkarray, binary operations, control structures, 
etc. This last chunk is the most complicated and difficult to predict the flow of my
implementation, but I'm hoping it will somehow intuitively come to me as I approach
this point in the project.

### What do you think will be the hardest part about this assignment?
I am afraid of a majority of this project. It feels very large and very daunting. The
concepts individually seem simple, but writing a cohesive project implementing all of
the requirements is hard to imagine. 

### How can you find solutions for the hardest part of the assignment?
I will use the approved resources and perhaps some research of typechecker 
examples if I get too overwhelmed. Additionally, I intend to keep in regular contact
with Dr. Flint in the case of questions that I cannot find answers to.
